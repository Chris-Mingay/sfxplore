TITLE
SFXplore

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Browse, tag and preview your sound effects. Layer them into custom mixes and export straight back to your library.

FULL DESCRIPTION
Find the right sound. Make it your own.

SFXplore is a desktop sound effects browser with a built-in mixer, made for game developers and sound designers working with their own audio collections.

Bring your sound folders together, preview effects as you browse, and use colored tags to keep useful sounds within reach. When one sound isn't enough, drag a few into the mix drawer, shape the layers, and export a new effect for your project.

EXPLORE YOUR COLLECTION
• Add local folders and browse their subfolders, or search across all your libraries.
• Preview WAV, OGG and MP3 files without opening a separate audio editor.
• Organize sounds with colored tags and combine multiple tag filters.
• Navigate with the keyboard, reveal files in your file explorer, or copy a sound to your project folder.
• Rescan when your collection changes and review what was added, changed or removed.

BUILD SOMETHING NEW
• Drag sounds onto a timeline with multiple tracks and clips.
• Move and trim clips, adjust volume, and shift pitch up or down by as much as two octaves. Pitch changes also affect playback speed and length.
• Shape fade-ins and fade-outs with editable volume curves and a choice of easing methods.
• Balance layers with track volume and master gain; mute tracks or audition individual clips and tracks.
• Undo and redo edits, and organize saved mixes in folders. Mix edits save automatically.

EXPORT AND KEEP CREATING
Export your mix as WAV or OGG at 48 kHz stereo. Save it to a project folder, or export directly into a sound library and have it indexed immediately.

YOUR ORIGINALS STAY INTACT
Trimming, pitch changes and volume edits happen inside the mix. SFXplore never edits your original audio files.

GETTING STARTED
Launch SFXplore, choose Add library, select a folder of sounds, and scan it. Click a sound to preview it. Open the Mix drawer and drag sounds onto the timeline to start building an effect.

Bring your own audio collection. Screenshots show an illustrative demo library.

WINDOWS DOWNLOAD / INSTALL INSTRUCTIONS
Extract the entire archive and run SFXplore.exe. Keep the executable, its data_SFXplore_* directory and the tools folder together. The standalone build does not require the Godot editor.

OPTIONAL TECHNICAL DETAILS
Import / preview: WAV, OGG and MP3.
Mix export: WAV (32-bit float) or OGG (Vorbis), 48 kHz stereo.
Library catalog, tags and mixes are stored locally.
Rescan folders to update the catalog; automatic folder watching is not currently supported.
